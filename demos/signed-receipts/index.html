Signed Receipts, Provable Spend

A settled purchase is signed over a canonical, fixed-field-order payload — {v, id, vendor, amount, currency, wallet, timestamp} — so the signature commits to exactly what happened, byte for byte. Sign a genuine receipt, then tamper one field at a time and watch which ones the signature catches. The canonicalization is the real canonicalPurchaseReceiptPayload from kcp-harness; the signing is genuine crypto.subtle Ed25519, right here in your browser.
